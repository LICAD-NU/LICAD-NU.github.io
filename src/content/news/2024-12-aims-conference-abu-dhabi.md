The **14th AIMS Conference on Dynamical Systems, Differential Equations, and Applications** took place from December 16–20, 2024, at the [Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre (ADNEC)](https://awm-math.org/event/the-14th-aims-conference/) in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, hosted by New York University Abu Dhabi. The conference brought together more than 1,500 participants from 60 countries.

## Key details from our team

- **Five LICAD members**, including the PI, took part in the conference.
- **Dr. Ardak Kashkynbayev** co-organized the session *Delay and Functional Differential Equations and Applications*, together with Professor Yang Kuang (Arizona State University) and Professor Fathalla Rihan (UAE University).
- Our Ph.D. students presented their research on topics including **synchronization analysis of neural network systems** and **traveling wave solutions**, and their talks received positive feedback.

Read more in the [Nazarbayev University news story](http://ssh.nu.edu.kz/tpost/gbt8d6z0a1-nazarbayev-university-shines-at-14th-aim) and on the [conference page](https://awm-math.org/event/the-14th-aims-conference/).
