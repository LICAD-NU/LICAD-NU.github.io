The **15th International ISAAC Congress** (International Society for Analysis, its Applications and Computation) was held at Nazarbayev University in Astana, Kazakhstan, from July 21–25, 2025, the first time the congress took place in Central Asia.

Our research group organized **Special Session 4: Beyond Nodes and Edges: The Power of Complex Networks in Modeling Real-World Systems**, and **four members of our group** participated in the session.

## About the session

**Organizers:** Ardak Kashkynbayev, Rakkiyappan Rajan, Balachandran Manavalan, and Jinde Cao

Complex networks are systems with many interconnected components exhibiting emergent behaviors. One intriguing phenomenon is synchronization, where nodes coordinate their behavior over time. The network topology, node dynamics, and coupling strength influence synchronization patterns. Studying synchronization provides insights into organizing principles of complex systems and has applications across domains like neuroscience, power grids, and communication networks. The session invited all presentations related to this topic.
