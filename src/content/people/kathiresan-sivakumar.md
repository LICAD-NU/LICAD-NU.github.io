Dr. Kathiresan Sivakumar (Ph.D., M.Sc.) completed his Ph.D. at LICAD (2022–2025). His research covers nonlinear dynamics, neural network synchronization, reinforcement learning, optimal control, secure communication, and image encryption.

**Expertise.** Lyapunov stability theory, event-triggered control, novel control techniques, cyber attacks, Takagi–Sugeno fuzzy systems, and reinforcement learning algorithms.

**Recent work.** Homotopy-based reinforcement learning for adaptive dynamic programming, including Homotopic and Bias Policy Iteration methods for unknown continuous-time and Markov jump linear systems, and neural-operator control of reaction–diffusion PDEs.

**Publications.** Peer-reviewed journal articles and conference papers, with further manuscripts under review and an accepted article.

**Technical skills.** MATLAB, Python, Jupyter Notebook, GitHub.

**Research goal.** To apply advanced control and synchronization methods to secure communication networks and biomedical systems, focusing on practical applications under cyber–physical constraints.
