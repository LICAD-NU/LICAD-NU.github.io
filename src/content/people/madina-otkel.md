Madina is a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate (2023–present) whose research focuses on chaos and complex systems, control theory, and the synchronization of neural networks. She holds both a Bachelor's and a Master's degree from Nazarbayev University, completing her M.Sc. at LICAD (2021–2023).

Throughout her academic journey, Madina has actively participated in international scientific conferences, presenting her research at several prestigious events, including the FAIA Conference in Antalya (October 2023), the DDEs-AIIDs Conference in Al Ain (November 2023), the Society for Mathematical Biology Conference in Seoul (July 2024), and the American Institute of Mathematical Sciences (AIMS) Conference in Abu Dhabi (December 2024).

Her research contributions include two peer-reviewed journal publications:

1. *Finite-time and fixed-time synchronization analysis of shunting inhibitory memristive neural networks with time-varying delays* (2022), published in Chaos, Solitons & Fractals.
2. *Finite-time/fixed-time synchronization of memristive shunting inhibitory cellular neural networks via sliding mode control* (2024), published in Mathematics and Computers in Simulation.

Beyond her academic pursuits, Madina is a proud mother of two wonderful children, a beautiful daughter and a son, who bring joy and inspiration to her life.
